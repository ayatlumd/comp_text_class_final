United Nations, 12 May (EFE)- The United States denounced on Wednesday at the UN China's alleged abuses of Uighurs and other minorities in Xinjiang Province and pledged to work to get Beijing to stop this "genocide."
"We will continue to get up and talk until the Chinese government stops its crimes against humanity and the genocide of Uighurs and other minorities in Xinjiang," U.S. Ambassador to the United Nations Linda Thomas-Greenfield said at the start of an event organized by the U.S. and some of its allies, along with human rights organizations, about the situation in this area of China.
Thomas-Greenfield said his country will continue to work alongside its allies and partners "until the Chinese government respects the universal human rights of all its people."
Last March, Joe Biden's government already accused China of "genocide" and "crimes against humanity" against Uighurs and other Muslim minorities in northwestern Xinjiang province in its annual human rights report, the first under this administration, maintaining a line already marked by Donald Trump's Executive.
The U.S. diplomat today denounced that people are being "tortured" in Xinjiang, "forcibly sterilizing women," and noted the existence of credible information that many Uighurs and people from other minorities are being subjected to forced labour by the state.
"Today we have listened and will continue to hear how the rights of Uighurs and other ethnic minorities in Xinjiang are being abused and raped to the extreme," Thomas-Greenfield insisted.
The event, led by the US, the United Kingdom and Germany, is scheduled to include human rights activists, experts and representatives from organizations such as Human Rights Watch (HRW) and Amnesty International, who have repeatedly denounced alleged Chinese abuses in Xinjiang.
Prior to the start of the appointment, HRW argued in a statement that UN member states should prompt an independent investigation into alleged crimes against minorities in the Chinese province.
Beijing has harshly criticized the convening of this meeting and called on UN countries to boycott it, ensuring that it responds to an attempt by the U.S. and other Western powers to use human rights as a tool to interfere in Chinese internal affairs.
