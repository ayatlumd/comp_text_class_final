With November 11, "Chinese Singles' Day," approaching, Internet users recently voted on the top 11 occupations that tend to cause people to stay single.1) Civil servantsAs China's social system has become more transparent and less corrupt in recent years, the occupation of civil servant is no longer as highly regarded (or lucrative) as it used to be. Most average civil servants receive official monthly salaries of just a few thousand yuan, with which they can hardly afford to buy an apartment, car or support their families.2) CouriersWith the rapid development of e-commerce, there are now approximately 2 million couriers across the country. Most are young migrant workers who spend their workdays, weekends and even holidays delivering parcels, of which they are paid per signature of recipient rather than a base salary.3) Flight attendantsThese often beautiful and shapely young women appear to lead glamorous, jet-setting lives traveling around the world. 
But for most stewardesses, all the people they meet on their flights, including the lonely men who hit on them, are just passersby in their life.4) WritersWith the development of China's digital platforms, physical books are on their way out, though, ironically, the net has become flooded with wannabe writers. As such, few true writers and authors can assure their quality life anymore by simply writing.Other occupations, including computer programmers, migrant workers, artists and even doctors, ranked among the top 11.From eastday.comFrom the thread "Chinese people's average lifespan increased"New research published by The Lancet shows that Chinese people's average lifespan has increased by 8.5 years compared with their average lifespan in 1990. The decline of infectious diseases, dysentery and infant mortality are among the reasons for prolonged lifespan.But the research also showed that prolonged lifespans differ according to region. In underdeveloped areas such as Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region, Qinghai Province and Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, the death rate has been raised due to more infectious diseases, traffic accidents, gastric cancer and cerebrovascular disease.According to the study, Shanghai residents have the longest lifespan across the nation. In 2013, Shanghai men's average lifespan was 80.2 years old and Shanghai women's lifespan was 85.2 years old, almost the same as Japanese and French citizens, who are known for having the longest lifespans in the world. The research report said that differences in lifespan by country reflect unbalanced economic growth, growing income gaps and lack of access to medical treatment.From online.sh.cn
