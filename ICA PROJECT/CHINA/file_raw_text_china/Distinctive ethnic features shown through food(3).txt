Tasting exotic and delicious food is a not-to-miss part of travelling in Xinjiang. In Emin county of Tacheng, a northwestern county in Xinjiang, the food variety is especially rich the area inhabits 25 ethnic groups, including the Han people, Kazak, Uyghur, Mongols, Hui people and Rusos.

Tasting exotic and delicious food is a not-to-miss part of travelling in Xinjiang. In Emin county of Tacheng, a northwestern county in Xinjiang, the food variety is especially rich the area inhabits 25 ethnic groups, including the Han people, Kazak, Uyghur, Mongols, Hui people and Rusos.
A group dinner event was held at Lin Zhongdong and Kurlux's homes on Wednesday with neighbors from different ethnic groups sharing their home-cooked dishes together.
Residents from Kazak, Mongol, Ruso, Uyghur and other areas prepared the most distinctive dishes from their ethnic groups. The whole party is a gathering of unique ethnic cuisines, along with enthusiastic singing and dances.
