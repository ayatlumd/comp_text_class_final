Two men were sentenced to death and three more were given prison terms on Monday over a deadly terrorist attack in the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region which killed 15 people in late April. The incident happened on April 23 in Bachu county, outside the city of Kashi. On that day at noon, local police officers and community workers detected suspicious behavior and dangerous devices at a home during a regular visit. 
Fearing the exposure of their terrorist group, Musa Hesen and Rahman Upur, the two main criminals who were given the death penalty on Monday, led the group to hack and burn to death 15 police officers and community workers. According to the Intermediate People's Court of Kashi, since March 2008, the terrorist group has promoted extremist religious ideas, and learned how to plan and organize terrorist activities through videos and audio recordings from overseas terrorists. They also carried out training for terrorist attacks and prepared knives, flags and self-made explosives to plan for terrorist attacks. In recent years, Xinjiang has witnessed several cases of violent attacks which claimed the lives of hundreds of people including women and children. The Chinese public has been unanimous in support of the verdicts for those terrorists. The violent attacks in Xinjiang shouldn't be confused with ethnic problems. The agitators of these violent attacks mainly targeted grass-roots organs in Xinjiang and killed innocent civilians, some of whom were Uyghur people or from other ethnic groups. Take the April 23 attack for example, those killed reportedly included 10 Uyghur people and two Mongols. This is a typical form of terrorism that deserves a severe crackdown.The Xinjiang government is leading the charge in realizing economic development in the region. They have recognized that economic development will create more job opportunities and create favorable conditions for improving livelihoods, stability and harmony among different peoples. There is no dispute that the Chinese public maintains a "zero-tolerance" attitude toward terrorists who incited killings, disturbed the order of the society and upset the locals. Terrorism is a common enemy of the whole world. The US waged the war on terror after the 9/11 attacks. No countries apply a policy of benevolence to terrorists but instead they maintain strong condemnation and heavy crackdowns. China must be resolute in battling against terror and punish terrorists in accordance with the law. That is a demonstration of rule of law, and follows the demands of all of society.
