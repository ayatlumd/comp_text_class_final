September is harvest season, and cotton in Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region is ripe for the picking. Looking far into the distance, the cotton fields are like a vast, boundless white ocean. At this time each year, a large population of farmers, mostly women from Gansu, Qinghai and Ningxia in the far west, and China's most populous province, Central China's Henan Province, descend upon Xinjiang like migratory birds to pick cotton. Since many cotton fields are in sparsely-populated areas, Xinjiang will need almost a million cotton pickers during the two-month harvest period, which runs through November.To meet the need for manual labor, local authorities in central and western China organize groups of farmers to travel thousands of kilometers to help pick cotton. Cotton pickers typically have to work over eight hours a day, and earn around 10,000 yuan ($1,428) for two months of work.Global Times
