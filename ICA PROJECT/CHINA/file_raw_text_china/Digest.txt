Outdoors
Ultimate challenge stops in Altay
When Xtrail combines with Adventure Racing World Series (ARWS), the result is guaranteed to be adventurous and exciting.
From Sept 25 to 28, the 2016 Xtrail Altay Expedition will be held in China's northwest tip of Altay, Xinjiang Uyghur autonomous region. Thirty-nine teams from 17 countries will face the 330-kilometer challenge of mountain biking (156km), cross-country running (104km), canoeing (34km), and rope skills (20km). Participants must navigate the course by themselves and the top five teams will win a cash prize.
This will be first time China has hosted ARWS, the world's top endurance and adventure race, which demands that participants compete day and night. It will also be the first time ARWS has come to Asia. National Geographic Channel will produce a one-hour special on the competition.
Mountain quest comes to Hezhang
A total of 2,100 people took part in the 2016 national mountain crossing quest which ended in Hezhang county in Guizhou province over the weekend.
The event, organized by the China Mountaineering Association, Guizhou Provincial Sports Bureau and Bijie Municipal People's Government, attracted 150 participants for the 50km run, 450 for the 20km run and 1,500 for the 6km fitness fun run.
Zhang Fenhong from Zunyi, Guizhou province won the women's 50km race in 6:8.30 and the men's title went to Duan Dongjie from Yunnan province in 4:8.22.
Soccer
Costa brace saves ragged Chelsea
Diego Costa scored twice, including an 81st-minute equalizer, as Antonio Conte maintained his unbeaten start to life as Chelsea manager in a 2-2 draw at Swansea City on Sunday.
The irrepressible Costa leveled with a deflected but spectacular overhead bicycle kick after Branislav Ivanovic's shot had been sent spinning in his direction.
The goal, Costa's fourth in as many games, spared Conte from his first loss in English soccer after two controversial Swansea goals in three second-half minutes had canceled out Costa's opener.
NFL
NFL probes medical response to hits
Carolina Panthers quarterback Cam Newton absorbed multiple hits to the head in the season-opening loss at Denver, and Broncos' players said in the aftermath of Thursday's game that hitting the reigning MVP was key to the game plan.
Newton's father, Cecil, said the family was "grossly disturbed" by the repeated shots to his son.
Now the NFL and NFL Players Association are investigating the response by medical personnel during the game. One specific hit - helmet-to-helmet contact initiated by Broncos safety Darian Stewart - prompted the compliance investigation.
Cycling
Athletes pedal off in Poyang Lake
The seventh Tour of Poyang Lake kicked off in Yichun, Jiangxi province, on Sunday.
Covering 11 cities in Jiangxi, the annual tour will this year last until Sept 23. More than 120 cyclists of 21 teams from 30 countries and regions, including Australia, France and the US will compete in the tour.
For the first time, a team from Africa will compete at the tour. The course length has increased to 1,358.7km from 1,080km last year.
